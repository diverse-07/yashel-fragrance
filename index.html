Fragrance Formulation

The Olfactory Pyramid: 24-Hour Evolution

Like fine musical notes, a master fragrance reveals itself in successive tiers. By using high pure oil ratios, Yashel perfumes project seamlessly throughout the day.

No Premature Evaporation

Heavy resinous base fixatives hold the scent onto skin and fabric.

Traditional Kannauj Hydro-Distillation

Copper stills preserve the subtle, natural aromatic molecules.

01. Top Notes · 0 – 30 Minutes Opening

Calabrian Bergamot, Oceanic Breeze & Fresh Mint

First impression: vibrant, clean, and refreshing.

02. Heart Notes · 30 Mins – 6 Hours Core Body

Taif Damascena Rose, Kashmiri Saffron & Cardamom

Signature character: romantic, warm, and sophisticated.

03. Base Notes · 6 – 24+ Hours Resonance

Cambodian Dehn Al Oud, Mysore Sandalwood & Amber

Lingering trail: regal, intimate, and unforgettable.
