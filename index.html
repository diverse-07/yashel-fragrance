Artisanal Atelier

The House of Yashel: Craftsmanship in Greater Noida

Founded in Greater Noida by Ankit Goyal and Ishani Pandit, Yashel Fragrance was built on a direct premise: fine fragrance should be highly concentrated, long-lasting, and skin-safe without commercial shortcuts.

Combining traditional Kannauj copper hydro-distillation with modern perfume chemistry, Yashel crafts pure non-alcoholic attars and extraits formulated for enduring presence.
