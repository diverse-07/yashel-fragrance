At Yashel Fragrance, every formulation follows a precise architectural ratio. We do not dilute with excess alcohol or artificial dispersants. The fragrance unfolds systematically across your skin over the span of a day.

12 to 24 Hour Longevity

Engineered to outlast heat and movement, maintaining pure sillage from dawn till dusk.

Certified IFRA Skin Safety

Artisanal Atelier

The House of Yashel: Craftsmanship in Greater Noida

Founded in Greater Noida by Ankit Goyal and Ishani Pandit, Yashel Fragrance was established with a singular conviction: luxury perfumery must be authentic, highly concentrated, and devoid of synthetic commercial dilution.

By merging the ancient copper hydro-distillation traditions of Kannauj with contemporary French perfume formulation techniques, Yashel produces long-lasting, skin-safe blends that evoke confidence, royalty, and timeless grace.
